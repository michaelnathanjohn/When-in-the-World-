Borders for 1800, 1815 and 1880, and the land backdrop

Historical Basemaps by André Ourednik and contributors. github.com/aourednik/historical-basemaps

Simplified and renamed for this game in globes-historical-basemaps.js, shared under the same licence. There is no free worldwide border data for 1816–1877, so the game has no globes for those years.

Colonies and independent countries (the stripes)

Striped areas were colonies, protectorates, mandates or other territories controlled from outside; the ruler is shown in brackets. From 1886 on this comes from the status recorded in CShapes 2.0. For 1800, 1815 and 1880 it follows Gleditsch, K. S. & Ward, M. D. (1999), "Interstate System Membership: A Revised List of the Independent States since 1816," International Interactions 25(4), 393–413, and the Correlates of War Project's State System Membership list. Kingdoms and peoples that governed themselves but are not on those lists (for example Asante, Sokoto or Aboriginal nations) are not shown as colonies. Areas recorded as "occupied" are striped without naming a ruler.

Privacy

The game uses no cookies and no advertising trackers. Your streak, stats and progress are kept in your own browser on this device.

When you finish the daily set, your five scores, the date and a random code for this device are sent to our scoreboard so we can show the daily averages. The code is not linked to your name, email or any account, and only running totals for each day are kept, not individual results.

This game is not affiliated with or endorsed by ETH Zürich or the Historical Basemaps project. Map borders are simplified and approximate, and borders in disputed areas are shown as recorded in the source data, not as a statement on any dispute.
